# Design System

## Style
Minimal, Functional, High-Contrast

## Colors
Primary: #4F46E5 (Indigo)
Success: #22C55E (Green for completed)
Danger: #EF4444 (Red for missed)
Background: #F8FAFC
Card Background: #FFFFFF
Text: #0F172A

## UX Requirements
- One-tap quick logging from the dashboard.
- Inactive/completed habits should visually recede (dim or collapse).
- Clear empty states when no habits are due today.
